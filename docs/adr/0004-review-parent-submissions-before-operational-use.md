# Review parent submissions before operational use

Parent-submitted registrations remain in a separate "Awaiting approval" queue until staff check and approve them. Pending child and adult details and relationships must not become available for collection matching or place the child in the normal child list before approval, so unreviewed parent submissions cannot establish operational collection records.

Approval of parent registrations and proposed details updates requires the separate approval permission and the reviewer's existing site access, as recorded in ADR 0008.

Submission can enter this queue while other nominated parents or guardians still need to accept required documents. Approval is blocked until every nominated adult has personally accepted all required documents, with outstanding acceptances visible to staff, as detailed in ADR 0006.

Staff can return a registration for corrections with a message explaining what needs changing. Send the invited adult a fresh secure link and retain the submitted details for editing and resubmission. The returned registration stays excluded from normal operational use until it is resubmitted and approved; this correction flow does not reuse the closed original invitation.

Resolve possible duplicate adults and children during staff approval. Show staff possible matches and require an explicit decision to link an existing person or create a different person. Matching names and dates of birth must not automatically merge records. Parents cannot search a directory of existing nursery records. This extends the explicit duplicate-resolution approach in ADR 0002 to parent submissions; access to possible matches must respect existing staff site boundaries.

Registration approval and a child's active/inactive status are separate concepts. Approval makes the registration available for normal use; active/inactive records whether the child's attendance continues, pauses, or ends. Active status is set separately for each site, so ending attendance at one nursery does not change attendance at another.

Approving a parent-submitted registration makes the child active at the inviting site by default. Staff can instead choose "Approve as inactive", including when registration is completed ahead of attendance. Pending registrations remain excluded from normal use regardless of the active status chosen at approval.

Inactive children are excluded from automatic collection matching at that site. Staff can find their retained records through an "Inactive" filter and reactivate them when attendance resumes; their history is retained.

At introduction of this workflow, existing children start as approved and active at their current sites to preserve current operation. Staff can then mark leavers inactive. Existing families are not forced through registration again, and missing contact details can be completed separately. This does not create historical document acceptances.

This decision concerns parent submissions. The existing complete staff registration flow in ADR 0002 remains a separate workflow.
