# Parent registration through an invitation without an account

Parents complete the initial child registration through an expiring, signed invitation link sent to the adult email address entered by staff, followed by a check against the child's date of birth entered by staff. No parent account is required, keeping the initial registration flow simple; the submitted registration awaits staff checking and approval.

ADR 0006 extends the agreed scope to staff-initiated requests for adults to accept updated site documents after registration, using individual signed links and an adult DOB check. The initial registration flow still requires no parent account.

The scope also includes an adult page after registration, showing that adult's linked children and allowing details to be updated. Adults receive a permanent, bookmarkable signed return link, but opening a new session requires fresh email verification through a short-lived access link. The permanent URL alone does not grant access to family details. ADR 0007 records this access boundary; this does not make the initial registration invitation reusable.

Each invitation is valid for seven days from sending. Staff can resend a fresh invitation, which starts a new seven-day validity period and invalidates the previous link. Successful submission closes the invitation immediately so it cannot be reused, including to reopen or edit the submitted form. The submission confirmation must tell the adult that the registration link is no longer valid. Reopening a used link shows an "already submitted" message without revealing registration details. Completing fields or encountering validation errors does not consume the invitation; closure follows successful submission.

Five failed date-of-birth attempts lock the invitation. Staff can issue a fresh link to restore access. The registration page reveals no child or adult details until the child's date of birth has been verified.

The child's date of birth entered by staff is fixed throughout registration and cannot be changed by the parent in the form. If it is wrong, staff correct it and issue a fresh invitation, keeping the access-verification value separate from parent-editable details.

Parents can save a registration draft and return before submission through the same invitation and child DOB check while the invitation remains valid. Drafts remain separate from submitted registrations and operational child, adult, and relationship records. Saving a draft does not consume the invitation.

If an invitation expires, resending to the same adult at the same email address preserves the saved draft while invalidating the old link. Changing the recipient email must not automatically expose the existing draft to the new recipient.

The first version is a collection registration form, rather than full nursery enrolment. It collects child and adult names and dates of birth, optional photos and alternative names, adult contact details, adult-child relationships and collection permissions, and acceptance of the site's required documents. Names and dates of birth are required for the child and every adult, including additional adults. The invited parent must provide an email address and phone number; every additional adult must have a phone number. Email is also required for any additional adult nominated as responsible for accepting documents under ADR 0006; it is optional for other additional adults. Addresses, medical details, and broader nursery enrolment or emergency information are outside this version.
