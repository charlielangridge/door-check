# Separate registration approval from invitation access

Assigned nursery staff who can currently add children can send parent registration invitations. Approving parent-submitted registrations and proposed details updates requires a separate approval permission in addition to access to the relevant nursery. Invitation access alone must not confer approval authority, and approval permission must not broaden the reviewer's permitted sites.

This separates inviting families from accepting their details into operational use. Assign the approval permission through the existing role and permission system rather than introducing a role-name shortcut or bypassing site access checks.

Publishing site documents and sending document-update requests require a separate document-management permission, together with access to the relevant nursery. Registration approval authority does not grant document-management authority. This allows registration review to be delegated without granting control over the documents families must accept.
