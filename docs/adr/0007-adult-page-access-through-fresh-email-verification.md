# Adult page access through fresh email verification

After registering a child, an adult has a personal page showing their linked children and allowing details to be updated. Provide a permanent, bookmarkable signed return link, with fresh email verification through a short-lived access link required for each new session. Possession of the permanent URL alone must not reveal family details or permit updates. This provides returning access without password creation or account signup while avoiding indefinite access through a copied permanent URL.

The adult page becomes available immediately after successful registration submission, showing the submitted registration and its status. New staff-granted child-management rights take effect only after approval. Previously approved children remain available under the adult's existing permissions; a pending submission does not grant access to other existing family records.

Changes submitted through the adult page require staff approval before taking effect. Keep the current approved details in operational use until staff approve the proposed changes, and show the adult that the update is awaiting review. Document acceptances are recorded immediately rather than waiting for staff approval.

Only staff-designated parents or guardians may propose changes to a child's details or manage that child's linked adults. Other collectors may propose updates to their own details but cannot change the child or manage other adults. Collection permission alone grants no editing rights. Child-management rights are a separate designation from collection permission and document responsibility.

When staff approve registration, the adult originally invited to register the child becomes a managing parent or guardian by default. Staff can change that designation during review. Additional adults receive child-management rights only through an explicit staff decision.

Child-management rights are granted separately for each site. Approval to manage a child at one nursery does not grant management rights at another. The adult page can show the adult's linked children, with editing options reflecting the relevant site's approval.

Removing an adult from a child through site review ends that site's association and management/document responsibilities only. It preserves the shared relationship and other nurseries' associations; the removed association must not qualify for collection at that site.

Names, dates of birth, and photos remain part of one shared person record across sites. Approval of a proposed person-details change by one nursery updates that shared record for all nurseries, with a recorded approval and notification to other affected nurseries. Attendance, document acceptance, and child-management rights remain site-specific. This keeps a consistent person identity rather than separate conflicting profiles at each nursery.

Collectors without child-management rights see a limited child summary: name, photo, nursery, and their own relationship and collection permission. They can see and propose updates to their own details, but cannot see the child's date of birth or other adults' personal details.

Managing parents or guardians can see the child's full details and linked adults' names, photos, contact details, relationships, and collection permissions within their management scope. An adult's date of birth is visible only to that adult and authorised staff, not to other managing parents or guardians.

Adult-page email access links are single-use and expire after 15 minutes. An adult-page session ends after 30 minutes of inactivity, with a visible warning before expiry; fresh email verification is required to sign in again. The permanent return link remains bookmarkable.

Changing an adult's email requires verification of the new address and staff approval before access moves to it. Keep the existing email in use until both requirements are complete. On completion, invalidate old access links and sessions because email controls access to the adult page.

Each adult with online access must have a separate email address; two adults cannot share an online-access email. Email access identifies the individual's adult page and personal document acceptances. Collectors without email can still be recorded without online access.

A nursery can immediately suspend an adult's access to its child records, including access through existing sessions, and explicitly restore that access later. Suspension preserves the records and access granted by other nurseries. It is independent of the relationship's collection permission and does not automatically change it.

Registration invitations and document-only acceptance links retain their separate agreed access rules.
