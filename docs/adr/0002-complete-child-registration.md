# Complete child registration from the staff screen

Staff registering a child must link at least one existing adult or add a new adult, with a recorded relationship to the child. Save the child, any newly created adults, and relationships together rather than persisting the child first, so an interrupted or failed registration leaves no incomplete new records. This requires staff to complete the related-adult details before saving instead of returning to them later.

The existing-adult picker searches only adults belonging to the current venue. Staff must not gain access to other venues' adult records through child registration.

When a new adult's name and date of birth match an existing adult at the venue, show the existing adult's photo and details and offer "Link this adult" or "Create a different person". A possible duplicate requires an explicit choice rather than an automatic merge or an unconditional block, because distinct people can share those details.

Apply the same name and date of birth check to children at the current venue, offering "Open existing child" or "Create a different child" before creating a possible duplicate.

Photos are optional for both the child and newly added adults. Offer both "Upload image" and "Take photo"; lack of a photo or camera must not prevent registration. Existing collection checks continue to handle missing photos.

Place "Add Child" alongside the child count above the panels. It opens registration in the right-hand panel, including the ability to link existing adults or enter new adults and their relationships before saving. Follow the existing person forms: names and dates of birth are required, alternative full names are optional, and each linked adult requires a relationship to the child. Assigned venue staff can use this flow under the existing venue access boundary.

If staff cancel or select another child while the registration has unsaved details, ask "Discard this registration?" with "Keep editing" and "Discard". Discarding abandons the child and every adult entered in that registration without saving new records.
