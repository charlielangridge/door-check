# Child Collection

Matching collection requests to the adults and children recorded by staff.

## Language

**Staff collection board**:
An always-on display used by staff to review and resolve incoming collection requests on a wall-mounted kiosk tablet.

**Preparing child**:
The stage of an individual child collection in which staff are preparing that child for handover to the adult.

**Child collected**:
The completed outcome of an individual child collection, recording that the child has physically been handed over to the adult.

**Venue kiosk account**:
A staff account associated with one venue and linked to an individual staff member, used to operate the staff collection board. Actions identify the signed-in account; this alone does not establish which person physically used a shared tablet.

**Collection request**:
A request at exactly one site, naming the adult seeking to collect one or more children and the children they wish to collect. Its site is fixed when the request is created.

**Collection status screen**:
The screen shown to the collecting adult after submission, reporting the progress of their collection request.

**Child collection**:
An individual child's part of a collection request, with its own checks and progress through preparation to collection or cancellation. Children within the same request can progress independently.

**Request reviewed**:
The status shown to the collecting adult for a child after staff explicitly confirm that child's identity, photo, and relationship checks, including any recorded exceptions. Opening the request or completing automatic matching alone does not constitute review.

**Site membership**:
An association recording that an adult or child belongs to a site. A person can belong to more than one site.

**Child registration**:
Recording a child as belonging to a venue with at least one adult connected by a recorded adult-child relationship.

**Registration invitation**:
A staff-issued invitation for an adult at a specified email address to submit a child's registration, identifying the child by name and date of birth.

**Registration submission**:
The child details, adult details, relationships, and acceptance of the site's required documents supplied by the invited adult for staff review.

**Registration draft**:
An unfinished registration saved by the invited adult for completion before submission. It is separate from a submitted registration and does not establish operational child, adult, or relationship records.

**Required site document**:
A document belonging to a site that the invited adult must accept as part of registration, such as terms and conditions.

**Document acceptance**:
A record identifying the adult who personally accepted a particular version of a required site document and when they accepted it, covering all that adult's children at that site. It refers to that retained version even if the site later replaces the document; acceptance does not transfer to another adult or site.

**Document-responsible adult**:
A nominated parent or guardian responsible for accepting a site's required documents, with an email address for document requests. Being authorised to collect a child does not by itself make an adult responsible for documents.

**Adult page**:
A personal page through which a registered adult can view their linked children and update details.

**Proposed details update**:
A change submitted through an adult page that awaits staff review and approval. Current approved details remain in use until the change is approved; document acceptance is recorded separately and immediately.

**Managing parent or guardian**:
A parent or guardian designated by staff at a particular site to propose changes to a child's details and manage that child's linked adults there. This designation is granted independently at each site and is separate from collection permission and responsibility for accepting documents.

**Adult access suspension**:
A site's immediate suspension of an adult's access to its child records, which staff can explicitly restore. It preserves the records, access granted by other sites, and the separate collection-permission setting.

**Awaiting approval**:
The state of a submitted registration that has not yet been checked and approved by staff.

**Returned for corrections**:
The state of a registration staff have sent back to the invited adult with a message explaining required changes. The retained submission can be edited and resubmitted, but remains unavailable for normal use until staff approve it.

**Registration approval**:
Staff confirmation that a submitted registration has been checked and can be used in normal child and collection workflows.

**Active child**:
A child currently attending or expected to attend at a particular site, as distinct from a child whose attendance there has paused or ended. Active status is separate from registration approval and is set independently at each site.

**Inactive child**:
A child whose attendance at a particular site has paused or ended. Their record and history remain available to staff, but they are excluded from automatic collection matching at that site until reactivated.

**Also known as name**:
An alternative full name recorded by an administrator for a particular adult or child to help identify that person from a collection request or staff search. A person can have multiple such names while retaining their primary name.

**Collection request evidence**:
A requester photo and optional device location accompanying a collection request. The photo is required when the form has a working camera preview; a request can be submitted without it when camera access is unavailable, denied, or fails.

**Requester photo**:
A photo of the adult seeking to collect the child, supplied as collection request evidence.

**Automatic match**:
An identification of an adult or child belonging to the collection request's site, accepted without staff confirmation because it meets the agreed confidence and ambiguity criteria. Each person can be matched independently; people belonging only to other sites are excluded from automatic matching.

**Matched collection request**:
A collection request with an accepted adult identification, accepted identifications for its requested children, and an existing recorded relationship authorising the adult to collect each child. An exception authorized through a relationship override remains distinct from a recorded relationship match.

**Ambiguous match**:
An adult or child identification with more than one candidate meeting the automatic matching threshold. Staff approval is required even when one candidate scores higher than the others.

**Recorded adult-child relationship**:
An existing association between an adult and a child, shared across sites, recording their relationship and whether the adult is authorised to collect that child. It counts towards a matched collection request only when collection is authorised and both people belong to the request's site.

**Authorised to collect**:
An explicit yes/no permission on an adult-child relationship stating whether that adult may collect that child. Recording a relationship alone does not grant collection permission.

**Relationship override**:
An explicit staff decision to permit a single child collection when the identified adult and child have no recorded relationship, supported by a required explanation. Any authorized staff collection board user can make this decision; it does not create or change a recorded adult-child relationship and cannot bypass a recorded relationship's collection permission being off.

**Match requiring approval**:
A proposed identification that needs staff approval before it is accepted.

**Match candidate**:
A proposed identification of an adult or child, retained with its confidence percentage for evaluation or staff approval. A candidate is separate from an accepted match.

**Partial match**:
A child collection in which either the shared adult or that child has an accepted identification while the other person remains unresolved. Other children in the request can have different matching outcomes.

**No match found**:
The outcome of a search with no plausible candidates for the person being identified. This is distinct from a matching service failure and from the progress of other children in the same request.

**Automatic matching threshold**:
The minimum confidence percentage required to accept an individual identification automatically. The same threshold applies separately to the adult and child.

**Adult match confidence**:
The confidence percentage assigned to identifying the named requester as a particular recorded adult. It is retained separately from the child match confidence.

**Child match confidence**:
The confidence percentage assigned to identifying the requested child as a particular recorded child. It is retained separately from the adult match confidence.
