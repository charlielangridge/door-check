# Child Collection

Matching collection requests to the adults and children recorded by staff.

## Language

**Staff collection board**:
An always-on display used by staff to review and resolve incoming collection requests on a wall-mounted kiosk tablet.

**Preparing child**:
The stage of an individual child collection in which staff are preparing that child for handover to the adult.

**Child collected**:
The completed outcome of an individual child collection, recording that the child has physically been handed over to the adult.

**Venue kiosk account**:
A staff account associated with one venue and linked to an individual staff member, used to operate the staff collection board. Actions identify the signed-in account; this alone does not establish which person physically used a shared tablet.

**Collection request**:
A request at exactly one site, naming the adult seeking to collect one or more children and the children they wish to collect. Its site is fixed when the request is created.

**Collection status screen**:
The screen shown to the collecting adult after submission, reporting the progress of their collection request.

**Child collection**:
An individual child's part of a collection request, with its own checks and progress through preparation to collection or cancellation. Children within the same request can progress independently.

**Request reviewed**:
The status shown to the collecting adult for a child after staff explicitly confirm that child's identity, photo, and relationship checks, including any recorded exceptions. Opening the request or completing automatic matching alone does not constitute review.

**Site membership**:
An association recording that an adult or child belongs to a site. A person can belong to more than one site.

**Also known as name**:
An alternative full name recorded by an administrator for a particular adult or child to help identify that person from a collection request or staff search. A person can have multiple such names while retaining their primary name.

**Collection request evidence**:
A requester photo and optional device location accompanying a collection request. The photo is required when the form has a working camera preview; a request can be submitted without it when camera access is unavailable, denied, or fails.

**Requester photo**:
A photo of the adult seeking to collect the child, supplied as collection request evidence.

**Automatic match**:
An identification of an adult or child belonging to the collection request's site, accepted without staff confirmation because it meets the agreed confidence and ambiguity criteria. Each person can be matched independently; people belonging only to other sites are excluded from automatic matching.

**Matched collection request**:
A collection request with an accepted adult identification, accepted identifications for its requested children, and an existing recorded relationship connecting the adult to each child. An exception authorized through a relationship override remains distinct from a recorded relationship match.

**Ambiguous match**:
An adult or child identification with more than one candidate meeting the automatic matching threshold. Staff approval is required even when one candidate scores higher than the others.

**Recorded adult-child relationship**:
An existing association between an adult and a child, shared across sites. It counts towards a matched collection request at any site to which both people belong.

**Relationship override**:
An explicit staff decision to permit a single child collection when the identified adult and child have no recorded relationship, supported by a required explanation. Any authorized staff collection board user can make this decision; it does not create or change a recorded adult-child relationship.

**Match requiring approval**:
A proposed identification that needs staff approval before it is accepted.

**Match candidate**:
A proposed identification of an adult or child, retained with its confidence percentage for evaluation or staff approval. A candidate is separate from an accepted match.

**Partial match**:
A child collection in which either the shared adult or that child has an accepted identification while the other person remains unresolved. Other children in the request can have different matching outcomes.

**No match found**:
The outcome of a search with no plausible candidates for the person being identified. This is distinct from a matching service failure and from the progress of other children in the same request.

**Automatic matching threshold**:
The minimum confidence percentage required to accept an individual identification automatically. The same threshold applies separately to the adult and child.

**Adult match confidence**:
The confidence percentage assigned to identifying the named requester as a particular recorded adult. It is retained separately from the child match confidence.

**Child match confidence**:
The confidence percentage assigned to identifying the requested child as a particular recorded child. It is retained separately from the adult match confidence.
